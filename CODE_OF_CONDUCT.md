# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors.

- Be kind and respectful. Assume good intent.
- Offer constructive feedback; avoid dismissive or punitive language.
- Focus on the work; keep conversations inclusive and professional.

Harassment or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open a confidential issue or email the maintainers.
